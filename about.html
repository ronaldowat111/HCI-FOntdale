The Story of Fontdale

A modern lifestyle mall at Arcadia Circle

Fontdale was founded by Franz Diego in 2023 and opened its doors to the public in 2025. Set within Arcadia Circle, it was imagined as more than a place to shop — a premium destination where retail, dining, and entertainment meet under one roof.

Today, Fontdale brings together the finest tenants across two distinct wings, joined by a signature glass bridge that has become the building's landmark.
